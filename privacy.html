Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ROSE NOIR Uploader (“the app”), operated by the ROSE NOIR project, handles information when it uses Google and YouTube services.

1. What the app does

The app is a private tool used only by the ROSE NOIR team. It uploads videos that the team produced itself to the team's own YouTube channel (ROSE NOIR ローズ・ノワール) and sets their title, description, tags, thumbnail and AI-content disclosure. It is not offered to other users.

2. YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Information the app accesses

  • With the account owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the scopes youtube.upload (to upload videos) and youtube.readonly (to read the channel name, to make sure it uploads to the right channel).
  • The app does not access, collect or store any information about viewers or other YouTube users, comments, subscribers or analytics.

4. How information is stored

The OAuth access token is stored only on the team's own computer, in the local user profile, and is used only to upload the team's videos. No data is sent to any server other than Google/YouTube, and no data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

5. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes the app's access.

6. Data deletion

The app keeps no personal data. Videos uploaded by the app can be deleted in YouTube Studio by the channel owner.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: please contact us through our Facebook page ROSE NOIR.
